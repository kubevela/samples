# Logos on the sample mugs

Each `mug-<name>.svg` embeds one logo file, unmodified, as a base64 `data:` image. The logos are trademarks of their owners and are used only to label the demo's stages.

| Mug | Logo | Source |
|---|---|---|
| `mug-kubevela.svg` | KubeVela icon | [`cncf/artwork` `projects/kubevela/icon/color/kubevela-icon-color.svg`](https://github.com/cncf/artwork/blob/main/projects/kubevela/icon/color/kubevela-icon-color.svg) |
| `mug-guidewire.svg` | Guidewire | [`cncf/landscape` `hosted_logos/guidewire.svg`](https://github.com/cncf/landscape/blob/master/hosted_logos/guidewire.svg) |
| `mug-aws.svg` | Amazon Web Services | [`cncf/landscape` `hosted_logos/amazon-web-services.svg`](https://github.com/cncf/landscape/blob/master/hosted_logos/amazon-web-services.svg) |
| `mug-gcp.svg` | Google Cloud | [`cncf/landscape` `hosted_logos/google-cloud.svg`](https://github.com/cncf/landscape/blob/master/hosted_logos/google-cloud.svg) |

The KubeVela logo falls under the Linux Foundation [trademark policy](https://www.linuxfoundation.org/trademark-usage) and the [CNCF brand guidelines](https://www.cncf.io/brand-guidelines/). The other three are the logos each company lists on the CNCF landscape; their own brand guidelines apply. For a public talk, check those before using the slides or recordings.

`mug.svg`, for any other stage, has no logo.
